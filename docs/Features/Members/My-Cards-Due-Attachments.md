# My Cards, Due Cards, and My Attachments

Three personal cross-board lists in the member menu, each filtered to
"things assigned to / owned by me" across every board, instead of having to
check each board individually:

- **My Cards** - every card across all your boards where you are a member.
- **Due Cards** - every card across all your boards that has a due date,
  sorted so overdue/soon-due cards are easy to find.
- **My Attachments** - every file attached to any card across all your
  boards.

## Where to find it

Click your **avatar/name (top left)** to open the member menu, then one of
**My Cards**, **Due Cards** (labeled with the calendar icon), or **My
Attachments**.

```
┌ (your avatar) ▾ ───────────┐
│ ▸ My Cards                 │
│ ▸ Due Cards                │
│ ▸ My Attachments           │
│ ▸ Starred                  │
│ ▸ Global Search            │
│ ─────────────────────────  │
│   All Boards                │
│   Public                    │
│   Archive                   │
└───────────────────────────────┘
```

## Steps to use it

1. Click your avatar/name at the top-left of any page.
2. Select **My Cards**, **Due Cards**, or **My Attachments**.
3. The page lists matching items from every board you belong to; click any
   card/attachment to jump straight to it (opens that card on its board).

## Prerequisites

- **My Cards** needs you to be added as a card member on at least one card.
- **Due Cards** needs cards with a [due date](../Date/Due-Date.md) set.
- **My Attachments** needs at least one file uploaded to a card you can see -
  see [Attachments](../Cards/Attachments/Attachments.md).

## Related

# All Boards and personal pages

## What it is

**All Boards** is the page that lists the boards you can open, sorted into
sections in a left menu: Starred, Remaining, Home, Templates, Archive, your
workspaces and one tab per organization and team you are in. Each section has
its own address, so it can be bookmarked or sent to a colleague. Access still
follows board membership and public/private visibility; this page only shows
what you may already open.

## How to use it

1. Open **All Boards** from the home control or the username menu.
2. Select the section, workspace or group in the left menu.
3. Open a board by its title.

The signed-in user's starred boards (`profile.starredBoards`) are kept in the
user's profile, public ones included, so they remain starred after a reload.
Starring does not grant private-board access or change board membership.
